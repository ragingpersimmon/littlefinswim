Stocking August 9, 2026

First residents: Amano shrimp are in

Pulled the assassin snails — decided I didn’t want a predator loose in the tank ahead of shrimp. Amano go in first, as cleanup crew and as a live water-quality test before anything more sensitive goes in. Corydoras next once the shrimp settle, giant betta last once the tank’s fully proven out. Plants are filling in well — the Ludwigia is colouring up nicely under the current lighting, and the swords are finally pushing new growth.

Troubleshooting July 14, 2026

Detritus worms, and what they were actually telling me

Noticed a small white worm bloom crawling the glass near the substrate line. Not harmful on their own, but a symptom — anaerobic pockets forming under the aquasoil where mulm had built up, plus a stretch of slight overfeeding while I was dialing in the CO₂. Cut feeding back, ran a gravel vac pass through the low-flow corners, and the population dropped off within a week. Note to future self: overfeeding shows up in the substrate before it shows up anywhere else.

Water chemistry June 6, 2026

Dialing in before anything goes in

Confirmed parameters before adding anything alive — GH 8, KH 6, pH 7.2, holding steady at 26.2°C. Repositioned the spray bar along the back pane, angled just under the surface, enough flow to move the whole column without flattening the Cabomba. Running the Fluval 207 for now; a 407 is on the list once the corydoras and betta actually show up and the bioload catches up to the plants.
