Stocking August 9, 2026

Amano shrimp added as first residents

Assassin snails were removed after it was decided that a predator in the tank ahead of shrimp would be unwise. Amano shrimp were added first as a cleanup crew and as an early indicator of water quality before more sensitive livestock. Corydoras are planned next once the shrimp have settled, with a giant betta introduced only after the tank has been fully proven stable. Plant growth is progressing well; the Ludwigia is showing stronger colour under the current lighting, and the swords are producing new growth.

Troubleshooting July 14, 2026

Observed detritus worms and their likely cause

A small white worm bloom was observed on the glass near the substrate line. The population was not harmful on its own, but it appeared alongside anaerobic pockets forming beneath the aquasoil where mulm had accumulated, together with a period of slight overfeeding while CO₂ was being adjusted. Feeding was reduced and a gravel vac pass was used in the low-flow corners. The population declined within a week. This suggests that overfeeding may appear in the substrate before it becomes obvious elsewhere.

Water chemistry June 6, 2026

Parameters stabilized before livestock was added

Parameters were confirmed before any livestock was introduced: GH 8, KH 6, pH 7.2, holding steady at 26.2°C. The spray bar was repositioned along the back pane and angled just under the surface to provide enough flow to move the entire column without flattening the Cabomba. The Fluval 207 remains in use for now; a 407 is planned once the corydoras and betta are added and the bioload increases to match the plants.
