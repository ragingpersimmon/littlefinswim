Troubleshooting July 14, 2026

Detritus worms, and what they were actually telling me

Noticed a small white worm bloom crawling the glass near the substrate line. Not harmful on their own, but a symptom — anaerobic pockets forming under the aquasoil where mulm had built up, plus a stretch of slight overfeeding while I was dialing in the CO₂. Cut feeding back, ran a gravel vac pass through the low-flow corners, and the population dropped off within a week. Note to future self: overfeeding shows up in the substrate before it shows up anywhere else.

Feeding August 28, 2026

How much fish food is the right amount?

Food should be matched to the fish and the feeding style of the tank. Bottom feeders such as corydoras, loaches and plecos may need food that reaches the substrate, while species like bettas and cichlids often do better with foods tailored to their own needs.

Lighting August 28, 2026

How much sun do fish need?

Most tropical aquariums do well with a consistent 6–10 hour lighting cycle. A timer is usually the easiest way to keep the light schedule steady without relying on manual switching.

Shrimp care August 28, 2026

What happened to my shrimp’s skin?

Failed molts can be tied to water chemistry and general habitat stability. GH and mineral balance matter for shell formation, and tank conditions such as stable water and suitable cover can make a big difference for shrimp.

Fish behaviour August 28, 2026

Why is my fish staying at the bottom of the tank?

When a fish hangs near the substrate, it is worth checking water quality first. Ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH and temperature are all useful clues, and oxygenation can become relevant if the fish is struggling to breathe comfortably.

Fish behaviour August 28, 2026

Why is my fish swimming at the top of the tank?

Surface gasping usually points to a problem with oxygen exchange or water movement. If multiple fish are gathering near the surface, improved aeration and better surface agitation are often the first things to address.

Water changes August 28, 2026

How often should you change aquarium water?

A regular water-change routine matters more than reacting to each symptom. Consistency, stable parameters and a look at the underlying cause usually produce better long-term results than chasing quick fixes.

Water quality August 28, 2026

Why is my aquarium water cloudy?

Cloudy water often comes from several possible causes, including bacterial blooms, a recent change or a disturbance in the substrate. Working backward from what changed usually gives a more reliable answer than treating the cloudiness as a single problem.

Filtration August 28, 2026

How do I know if my aquarium filter is big enough?

Filter size should be judged by biological capacity and the needs of the tank, not just a large GPH number. A well-matched filter supports the tank’s bacteria, water movement and overall stability.

Stocking August 28, 2026

How many fish can I put in my aquarium?

Stocking should be based on adult size, social needs, territory and the biological load of the tank. A slower, more careful approach usually leads to a more stable aquarium.

Plants August 28, 2026

Why are my aquarium plants turning brown?

Brown plants often point to a mix of light, nutrient and carbon issues. The balance between these factors matters more than assuming one single cause is always to blame.
